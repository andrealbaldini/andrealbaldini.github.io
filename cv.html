Biography

Andrea L. Baldini is a philosopher by training (PhD, Temple University, 2014; PhD, University of Siena, 2011) working at the intersection of aesthetics, philosophy of law, and urban studies. His research asks how creative expression in public space is defined, valued, protected, and governed: what makes street art and graffiti what they are, how copyright shapes authorship, and how institutions decide what stays on the wall. He is the author of A Philosophy Guide to Street Art and the Law (Brill, 2018) and has published in journals including The British Journal of Aesthetics, The Journal of Aesthetics and Art Criticism, and Philosophy Compass. Current projects address street art in museums, the management of spontaneous public art, and art and design in the age of AI.
